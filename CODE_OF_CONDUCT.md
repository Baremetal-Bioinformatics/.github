# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique claims and implementations, not people.
- Welcome correction, negative results, and requests for stronger evidence.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and responsible disclosure.
- Clearly distinguish measured facts from hypotheses and opinions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, deliberate misrepresentation, publication of private information, or other conduct that makes participation unsafe is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to an organization owner through GitHub. Organization maintainers may edit or remove content, limit participation, or ban contributors when necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
